# Disclosure & Approval Policy - EP051

## 1. Disclosures
- **Public Disclaimer**: All public analytics must be accompanied by the standard regulatory disclaimer: "Past performance is not indicative of future results. Hypothetical or simulated performance results have certain limitations."
- **Methodology Transparency**: The methodology policy and versions must be accessible to users to ensure transparency in how metrics like "Drawdown" are calculated.

## 2. Retention
- **Aggregated Analytics**: Retained indefinitely for historical tracking.
- **Raw Trades**: Retained for 7 years to support reconciliation and compliance audits.

## 3. Approvals (Mocked for WF-005)
- **Compliance Officer**: Approved. Disclaimers are adequate.
- **Security Lead**: Approved. Isolation of non-DNA data is sufficient. Data classification aligns with corporate policy.
