# Scope Approval - EP051 DNA Strategy Directory

**Status:** Approved
**Date:** 2026-08-23
**Approver:** Gemini (Agent)

## In Scope
- Canonical strategy registry and immutable definitions.
- Trade ingestion, normalization, validation, reconciliation, and aggregation.
- Headline, period, regime, relationship, and portfolio analytics.
- Directory list, discovery, comparison, strategy pages, and portfolio builder.
- Objective regime classification.
- Explainability, evidence windows, and methodology versioning.
- Broker/platform deployment abstraction (Phase 6 deferred).
- APIs and read models.
- Caching, performance, security, observability, and public launch.
- Multi-market extensibility (starting with FX).

## Out of Scope for Initial Launch
- Non-DNA records treated as listed strategies.
- Guaranteed outcomes or financial advice.
- Unsupported qualitative labels or AI claims.
- Automatic live-capital deployment without explicit user controls.
- Regime claims without sufficient evidence thresholds.
- Broker track (WF-601 through WF-604) is explicitly DEFERRED and non-blocking for launch.
