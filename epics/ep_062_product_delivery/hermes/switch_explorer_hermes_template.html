How this works

Choose a market, product, ranking rule and hour. The app selects one strategy, compares alternatives, then shows a historical switch-versus-hold replay. Data from is embedded in this file; only information available at the decision time is used to select.

Where this comes from

This explorer is a simplified, static version of the strategy directory's Scenario & Criteria Universal Engine, the full interactive tool that ranks strategies, replays a day and follows the leaders. The screen below is that tool: forex, GBPAUD_C, Friday 9 October 2026, replayed to 14:45. It is one example, not the data in this file.

  • Return (top left): Net Return or Alt Net. Here this is the Net | Alt net switch.
  • Scenario, day, type, product: how strategies are picked (here Top Buy Sell, forex, GBPAUD_C). Here these are the asset class, product and scenario choices.
  • Replay and baseline: the day is replayed from 00:00 to a replay head (14:45); only snapshots at or before that time are used. Here this is the hour you choose.
  • Select top #1 at a time, auto-add every 15 minutes: each new leader is added to the same chart. Here this is the idea behind switching only when a better strategy appears.
  • The chart: each line is one selected strategy's cumulative net return through the day; the marked points show when it became #1. Sharp steps are large moves in the cumulative net.

What is different here: one file, no server, a fixed data time, crypto and forex kept separate, and the result shown as a plain choose, compare and switch flow.

What do “net”, “win rate” and “__MIN_WORD__ closed positions” mean?

Net is the platform’s cumulative result in its own units, including open and closed positions. Win rate is the share of closed positions with a positive result. The selection rule requires at least __MIN_WORD__ closed positions at the decision time. Similar rows below that minimum are context only—not eligible to select or switch to.

2 · Compare alternatives

How are the four comparisons defined?
Similar: same product, one setting differs. Opposite: five-minute equity changes correlate at -0.2 or lower. Overall: highest net on this product. Win rate: highest share of closed positions with a positive net. Each switch-eligible candidate must be a different strategy with a different net from the selected one. Identical results are clones. If no eligible candidate exists, the app says so; it never substitutes the selected strategy. Ties go to the lowest model id.
